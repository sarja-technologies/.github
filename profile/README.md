# Sarja Technologies

**Technology from device to datacenter.**

Sarja Technologies is an Australian technology company that designs, builds, supplies, secures and operates software, AI systems, infrastructure and workplace technology.

Founded and led by **[William Millena](https://github.com/crossainthero-lab)**.

## What we do

- **Software & systems** — Applications, integrations, data platforms and purpose-built software.
- **Artificial intelligence** — AI engineering, private AI infrastructure and deployment through [Sarja AI](https://ai.sarja.dev).
- **Cloud & infrastructure** — Platforms, networks, computing environments and their operations.
- **Cybersecurity & managed technology** — Security, reliability, support and ongoing system management.
- **Hardware & devices** — Device programs, deployment and lifecycle support.

## Software and engineering

**[AgentDock](https://agdc.dev)**  
A desktop workspace for Claude Code, OpenAI Codex and Google Antigravity. The free edition is available for macOS and Windows; AgentDock Pro is in developer preview. [Free edition source](https://github.com/crossainthero-lab/AgentDock).

**[Sarness](https://github.com/sarja-technologies/Sarness)**  
A self-hosted AI workstation with a Linux desktop client and GPU-hosted engine, developed for locally controlled AI workloads. [Repository and documentation](https://github.com/sarja-technologies/Sarness).

Our GitHub repositories include public engineering work alongside private development. They are not a complete catalogue of released products; see the official websites for current availability.
